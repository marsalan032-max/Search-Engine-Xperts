WHAT CUSTOMERS SAY

Reviews mention named engineers, same-day help with urgent jobs, tidy work and quotes that are kept.

Virtual-office address 27 Old Gloucester St is a widely used mail-forwarding address shared by many businesses. Google's rules don't allow a virtual office as a shown address, and profiles that use one can be suspended — taking every review with them.

The safe setup: a service-area business with the address hidden and your real areas listed (Holborn, Bloomsbury, City of London, Camden, Islington…).

One page for everything Your profile can only link to the homepage, which says one line about each service. Google compares the two to decide what you're relevant for.

Off-Page SEO

Off-page SEO is what the rest of the internet says about you. For a local plumber, accurate listings, steady reviews and a few trusted trade links beat hundreds of random links.

WhereStatusWhat to do
Leadstal, PoystFoundKeep name, phone and website identical
CheckatradeNot foundCreate and get vetted

Gas Safe RegisterCheckMake sure the listing links to your website
Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, YellCheckClaim and copy details from Google
THE GOLDEN RULEKeep one master sheet with the exact name, phone, website and hours, and copy every listing from it. Never buy reviews or offer rewards for them — it breaks Google's rules and can wipe all reviews from a profile.
