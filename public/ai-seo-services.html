Definition

What Is AI SEO?

AI SEO (also called Generative Engine Optimization or Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring a brand's content, data and entity signals so it can be understood, trusted and surfaced by AI systems such as Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, as well as in traditional search rankings.

These systems don't rank pages the way Google's classic algorithm does. They retrieve and synthesize information from sources they judge credible, then decide which ones deserve a citation, a mention, or a paraphrase. Earning that requires clear topical authority, machine-readable structured data, and strong entity and brand signals, along with backlinks and keyword placement.

Is this for you

Who Needs AI SEO?

Any business that depends on search for leads or sales has a stake in AI visibility. Here's how the opportunity typically shows up by industry.

SaaS Companies

Buyers research tools by asking AI assistants to compare options. Showing up in that comparison depends on clear, structured product and feature content.

Ecommerce Brands

Product and category pages need structured data and clear specifications so AI shopping answers can accurately describe and recommend what you sell.

Local Businesses

Location-based questions increasingly get AI-generated answers, so accurate and consistent business details across the web matter more than before.

Professional Services

Trust-driven decisions (legal, consulting, agencies) mean E-E-A-T signals and credible third-party mentions carry extra weight in AI-generated summaries.

B2B Companies

Long research cycles mean buyers ask AI tools multiple clarifying questions before contacting a vendor. Topical depth helps you appear at each stage.

Healthcare Companies

Accuracy and credibility signals matter heavily for health-related queries. Clear authorship, sourcing and structured data support (not replace) clinical review.

Financial Services

Regulated, high-trust queries reward well-sourced, clearly authored content, and entity clarity helps establish which brand is actually answering.

Global & Startups

Businesses scaling into new markets or launching from zero benefit from building entity and topical authority correctly from the start, in every target language and region.
